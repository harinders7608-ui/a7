Outerwear Disclaimer

Review the formal institutional commitments, client privileges, and operational protocols governing AviatorCoatUp.

1. Section 1 Provisions

The outerwear treatises, thermal insulative formulas, and leather care recommendations on AviatorCoatUp are presented solely for educational enrichment and historical interest. While our workshop conducts empirical windproof evaluations and sub-zero thermal trials, coat longevity involves diverse environmental factors, storage conditions, and variable maintenance routines. Patrons rely upon our leather care guides, temperature ratings, and conditioning protocols entirely at personal independent discretion.

2. Section 2 Provisions

Manual leather crafting, shearling skiving, heavy rotary cutting, and industrial post-bed sewing demand specialized machinery, ergonomic posture, calibrated manual tension, and supportive workshop routines. Informational materials published on this website do not replace certified occupational training in heavy leathercraft trades. AviatorCoatUp disclaims legal liability for personal injury, blade accidents, or material damage sustained while attempting referenced benchwork methodologies across workshops.

3. Section 3 Provisions

Digital representations, photographic imagery, and leather swatches presented across our online catalog are calibrated to reflect actual products as faithfully as possible. However, because natural sheepskin fleece and steerhide absorb botanical oils uniquely, minor discrepancies in shade and wool loft will inevitably occur. Ambient lighting conditions and monitor calibrations influence perceived tones. Patrons are encouraged to inspect physical hide swatches at our Manhattan studio.

4. Section 4 Provisions

This website may contain hypertext links directing visitors to third-party sheepskin tanneries, historical aviation museums, or independent toolmakers not operated by AviatorCoatUp. Such external references are provided solely as informational resources, and our organization does not endorse or assume responsibility for external web content. Visitors accessing third-party internet locations do so under the distinct terms and privacy protocols established by those independent operators.

5. Section 5 Provisions

For formal clarification regarding any workshop documentation, pelt provenance disclosures, or artisan accreditation standards, please submit written communication directly to our administrative office. You may direct inquiries to AviatorCoatUp Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or contact our patron advisory desk directly by telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We remain dedicated to providing transparent, verifiable craft information to all patrons and aviation outerwear connoisseurs.
