Privacy Policy

Review the formal institutional commitments, client privileges, and operational protocols governing AviatorCoatUp.

1. Section 1 Provisions

AviatorCoatUp operates our Manhattan leather atelier to craft bespoke shearling aviator coats, B-3 flight jackets, and vintage military outerwear. We collect minimal digital telemetry and contact records strictly to coordinate client commissions, manage workshop appointments, and answer outerwear inquiries. Our workshop complies fully with established global data protection frameworks, never selling or leasing your private contact records to commercial marketing entities across digital networks.

2. Section 2 Provisions

When you explore our outerwear collections, request custom shearling sizing commissions, or subscribe to our guild bulletins, we collect basic technical records including your IP address and browser type. If you place a deposit on a custom jacket or book a private consultation, we collect your name, postal address, email address, telephone contact, and payment details through encrypted gateways. All confidential transactional data remains protected within our secure New York records vault.

3. Section 3 Provisions

Our data processing operations rest upon legitimate artisan craft delivery, contractual fulfillment for commissioned goods, and your explicit consent for workshop bulletins. We retain client order records solely for the duration mandated by municipal accounting standards and lifetime warranty identification. Anonymized browsing telemetry is analyzed periodically to optimize website performance, refine mobile drawer navigation, and maintain stable server operation across all devices.

4. Section 4 Provisions

We implement robust security safeguards including Secure Sockets Layer protocols, layered network firewalls, and restricted credentials to protect your submitted records. Access to patron commission records is restricted strictly to authorized leather crafters managing workshop fabrication and logistics. While rigorous security protocols are maintained across our internal systems, internet transmissions carry inherent limitations, and clients are advised to protect personal account credentials.

5. Section 5 Provisions

Depending upon your place of residence, you hold recognized statutory rights concerning your personal records, including the right to inspect, correct, export, or request deletion of stored files. You may also object to automated data processing or revoke consent for promotional bulletins at any time without penalty. To exercise these individual privacy rights, submit a formal written request directly to our outerwear governance desk at our primary Mercer Street atelier.

6. Section 6 Provisions

Our leathercraft atelier is committed to resolving all client data inquiries with complete transparency and prompt diligence. Please direct your formal privacy communications to Data Protection Officer, AviatorCoatUp Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or telephone our support desk directly at +1-888-777-5845. We update this privacy policy periodically to reflect evolving data governance standards and artisan benchmarks across our worldwide craft network.
